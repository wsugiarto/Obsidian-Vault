- Expect all stages of the interview to have technical in it
	- technical can mean leetcode/coding, talking about projects, and system design (usually only big companies like faang)
- asking clarifying questions is very important
- Technical interviews are still mostly algorithmic, else there are sometimes now working with AI or system design
	- Being comfortable with mediums is the base line
- Expect to be able to get the optimal solution else bust
- Going faster is better as long as you still explain things
	- there are probably extensions to questions
- When you are completely clueless 
	- Dont just stay quiet and dont just throw random shit in
	- Just think out loud and ask the interviewer questions and theyll nudge or give you hints
